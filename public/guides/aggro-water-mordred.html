Game plan

Stock Water in the graveyard with Soaked Slash and early allies. Level to Flawless Blade so attacks float for memory. Use Deluge swings (Floodborne / Soaked) and fat Regulus / Striking Tides to race.

Opening checklist

  • Flawless Spirit → glimpse, keep cheap attacks and Fountain Bladehand.
  • L1 looks for an attack to banish/activate; put Eventide or Krustallan online.
  • Race to Flawless Blade — every spent attack becomes future memory.
  • Aurelian Regent closes; avoid Luxem main-deck cards so you don’t delevel.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting Mordred Bonus from Flawless Spirit — it does not enable those lines.
  • Leveling Aurelian while holding Luxem activations.
  • Ignoring Deluge thresholds before Floodborne Swing.
