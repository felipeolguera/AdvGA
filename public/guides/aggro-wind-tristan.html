Game plan

Put preparation on Tristan from Lookout / Outlook / Boots / Surveil. Swing with 2-cost Assassin attacks, then use Hired Blade draws and Shadowdancer shadows to finish.

Opening checklist

  • Spirit of Wind → Lookout / Outlook / Cloaked curve.
  • Tristan L1: take prep or Agility 3 depending on hand.
  • Materialize Gearstride Gloves + a 0-cost Sword when ready to attack.
  • Level Hired Blade with 2+ prep for card advantage; Shadowdancer closes.

Common mistakes

  • Resting Tristan every turn and starving Windwalker Boots.
  • Playing Combo Strike before level 2 (no wake-up).
  • Spending Corhazi’s prep lock when you needed the counters for Hired Blade.
