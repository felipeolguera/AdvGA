Game plan

Open with cheap Fire allies and Emberslash. Level to Blademaster for +POWER On Enter, fill the graveyard with Fire discards, then close with Blazing Lunge / Rending Flames and Ascendant Wings sword swarm.

Opening checklist

  • Spirit of Fire → curve Emberslash / Courier / Insurgent / Tindered.
  • L1 materialize Forgelight Blade, start attacking.
  • Level Blademaster when you can convert the On Enter pump into a kill.
  • Hold Ascendant Wings until you have Swords ready to swarm.

Common mistakes

  • Playing Dragon’s Dawn without three Fire in GY.
  • Burning every discard without leaving Fire for Stoked Slice / Firetongue.
  • Leveling Ascendant Wings with empty material swords.
