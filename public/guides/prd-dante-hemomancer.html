PRD Dante Hemomancer — Play Guide

Water / Exia Mage shell: Elysian allies, empower Aenean Spells, sacrifice and recover into Hemomancer.

Open AdvGA Lorraine guide
Load in AdvGA: Deck Builder → Load Dante (Water/Exia). Standard shell: Material 12 · Main 60 · Sideboard 15.

Game plan

Build an Elysian board of Exia allies, empower Aenean Spells, and use sacrifice/recover loops while Dante levels into Hemomancer. Water interaction protects the engine; Exia damage and empower close games.

Opening checklist

  • Spirit of Water → deploy free Elysian allies (Embryonic / Aspirant).
  • Level Dante for Mage Class Bonus; keep Heighten / Umbilical ready to empower.
  • Use Water wards and Frozen Shunt to survive until Hemomancer.
  • At L3, empowered Spell damage recovers life — lean into Rhesus / Frostlance / Blood Surge turns.

Common mistakes

  • Sacrificing your last Elysian Aura piece before a big Aenean Spell turn.
  • Empowering with nothing to cast — hold Heighten until a Spell is in hand.
  • Ignoring recover — Hemomancer wants you to deal empowered Spell damage, not only ally combat.
  • Flooding memory without Floating Memory / Fractal outlets.
